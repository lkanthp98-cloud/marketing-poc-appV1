Suggested business context for the marketing Genie space. Review with the business owner.

Use the curated Use Cases datasets. Keep native vendor delivery, synthetic paid cohorts,
banking acquisition and e-commerce populations separate. Do not join facts at different
grains without preaggregation and a documented attribution/allocation rule.

Always state the reporting period, currency, source population and relevant metric definition.
If the question omits essential scope, ask a clarifying question. Keep unresolved campaign
mapping statuses identifiable. Never infer a campaign match from similar names.

Vendor spend is platform-reported cost in its labeled currency. Do not sum currencies.
Platform conversions are not CRM applications or funded accounts. Reach is scoped to a
measurement window and is not additive across overlapping windows or scopes.

Rates are ratios of compatible summed numerators and denominators; do not average row rates.
Zero or missing denominators produce unavailable values, not zero-performance claims.
Submission funnel counts describe submissions with linked stages, not necessarily distinct
account/customer counts. Use one sales plan version when aggregating target actuals.

Platform spend, invoice amounts and GL postings are related representations of costs.
Compare them; do not add them as independent expenses. Retain signs of contribution proxies.
Synthetic observed relationship contribution is not audited revenue, incremental ROI or LTV.

Experiment conversion rates do not alone establish statistical significance. Brand survey
waves must be comparable before calculating lift. Attribution does not establish causality.
Do not manufacture missing KPI inputs or forecast values. Identify unavailable requirements.

Prefer aggregate results and select the columns required to answer the question.
Genie should use published data only; this app does not approve campaign mappings or modify data.
